resource budget: 48 semantic, 30 head and 38 journal-adapter methods. All 116
passed fresh, with no skips or source/runtime drift. The scoped static gate
passed with 117 unchanged historical type-baseline diagnostics and no new

type-safety claim. All 491 retained verifier inputs still matched at handoff.

```sh
.venv/bin/python -m devtools.verify --lanes fast --workers 4 \
  tests/test_candidate_http_semantics_v1.py \
  tests/test_candidate_http_head_v1.py \
  tests/test_candidate_http_observation_v1.py
```

Portable tests use generated inert fixtures and need no original runs or Docker.

journals, external checkpoints and audit anchors. The public origin manifest
alone does not contain those raw records; absent or changed evidence fails
closed. Historical inputs are reused as historical inputs, never counted as new
physical or independent-acceptance observations. The earlier 94 offline/9
