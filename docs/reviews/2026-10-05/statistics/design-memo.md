## Predeclare the pilot investment checkpoint

Use the complete pilot before selecting the next scope. The following are engineering investment rules, not significance tests and not frozen stop rules:

- If invariants, source provenance, or treatment fidelity fail: do not interpret efficacy or launch confirmation. Repair the specific violated requirement and retain the failed run. Do not automatically retry to replace it with a success.
- If both large arms achieve zero acceptance: the task/controller/model combination has a floor problem. Stop scale/infrastructure expansion; use the frozen development failure evidence to locate a bounded capability or policy failure. More independent samples of a broken common pipeline are unlikely to be the best next spend.
- If S16-G consistently stops or fails while O16-G completes under both assigned conditions: stop enlargement of this peer policy. The result is an actionable negative for this implementation on this family, even though it is not a population proof. Require a concrete mechanism and predeclared changed development test before further architectural investment.
- If all arms pass: the harness is feasible but the family has a ceiling for superiority. Preserve those results; do not weaken acceptance or change the pilot after seeing them. A separately selected development panel can estimate useful task difficulty.
- If S16-G shows credible completed-project or recovery advantage without violated invariants, or the contrast remains mixed: proceed only to a bounded, separately budgeted development panel or to held-out confirmation once the prerequisites below are genuinely ready. One family cannot estimate paired discordance reliably. A proposed 8–12 independent development families would provide mechanism variation and resource estimates, not proof of a 10pp advantage; its exact size and funding are unselected. All such families become permanently excluded from confirmation.

No hard threshold on the two related pilot conditions has calibrated population error. These rules decide whether to invest, not whether a universal hypothesis is true.

## Transparent power and interval calculations

The fresh calculations enumerate the multinomial distribution of paired outcomes using the retained conservative Bonferroni–Clopper–Pearson interval. They use no fitted pilot effect and no Monte Carlo. With W=swarm-only acceptance and L=orchestrator-only acceptance, delta=P(W)-P(L), discordance q=P(W)+P(L), and variance of each pair's signed outcome is q-delta². Enumeration conditions on W~Bin(n,pW), then L|W~Bin(n-W,pL/(1-pW)). Each benefit/exclusion probability was checked against the retained implementation (tolerance 1e-10); mutually exclusive benefit/equivalence/worthwhile-harm/unresolved categories sum to one. This consistency check is not independent formal statistical-code qualification.

The table uses one primary contrast, alpha .05, proposed margin 10pp. Benefit scenario: true +20pp, pW=.30, pL=.10. No-gain scenario: pW=pL=.15. Different columns concern different true scenarios.

| Independent families | Three-arm trajectories | Show >10pp gain when true gain=20pp | Exclude >10pp gain when true gain=0 | Establish ±10pp equivalence when true gain=0 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 30 | 90 | 0.6% | 1.2% | 0.0% |
| 100 | 300 | 8.3% | 10.4% | 0.0% |
| 200 | 600 | 25.9% | 32.8% | 0.0% |
| 400 | 1200 | 64.1% | 74.5% | 49.0% |
| 800 | 2400 | 96.1% | 98.6% | 97.1% |

These are planning probabilities, not a selected sample size, minimum, authorization, or empirically established assumptions. At 100 genuinely independent families a observed 30 swarm-only/10 control-only/60 tied split would give this method an approximately +1.4pp to +37.0pp interval: evidence of positive direction but not of >10pp benefit. With 15/15 discordant and 70 ties, the interval is approximately ±16.8pp, so no statistical difference does not establish ±10pp equivalence. Thirty all-tied independent families still give approximately ±13.6pp. The one-family pilot is dramatically weaker and cannot use an iid 6-run interval.

Discordance matters materially: under no gain at q=.10, 200 families have about 76.9% equivalence probability and 400 about 99.7%; under q=.30, the corresponding probabilities are about 0.01% and 49.0%. For a +20pp gain at q=.60, 800 families reach about 84.7% benefit power with this method. Full grids include alpha .025, boundary effects, and symmetric harm scenarios. A true gain exactly on the +10pp boundary should usually be unresolved about exceeding it.

The inherited conservative method spends substantial precision. Idealized normal calculations suggest the order of magnitude could be lower: for +20pp gain versus +10pp margin at q=.40, roughly 283 independent families at alpha .05 or 343 at alpha .025 for 80% power; no-gain exclusion at q=.30 gives roughly 236 or 286. These are NOT validated sample-size choices, coverage guarantees, equivalence power, or promised savings. Before buying hundreds of projects, qualify an established paired score/profile-likelihood or exact-unconditional/melded method, check sparse/boundary coverage and decision power, then choose a fixed design prospectively. The existing source references identify these alternatives; this memo does not implement or adopt them.
