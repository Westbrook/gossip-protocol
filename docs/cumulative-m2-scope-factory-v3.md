This successor makes the twelve authored M2 direct-API histories available to the cumulative scope factory and to a concrete prospective recipe → admission → original owner path. It also closes the storage batch-capture factory seam. The existing source, authority and recipe versions remain unchanged. These additions do not supply a complete independently approved ScopePlan, prerequisite qualifications, promotion authority or a physical candidate result.

Its closed factory roster contains the exact inherited CLI, HTTP, product-process, B01 and B02 families plus `m2-direct-api`. It reconstructs the original M2 definitions and their real observer selectors; caller-provided selector labels cannot extend that roster. All twelve M2 histories retain their 224 actions and 140 original comparisons, plus twelve whole-history mechanics guards. The current finite outputs provide 136 mapped outputs across 23 source units. Three reopen comparisons are mechanics only, and one further output has no semantic edge. Every missing unit and applicability cell stays in the declaration.

The M2 declarations support only the direct `public-contract` logical lane. A direct `Service.request` value does not establish wire HTTP behavior. Ordinary two-connection races do not establish a forced transaction schedule, and a same-process reopen is not a process crash. Native definition provenance and its original independent-acceptance purpose remain disclosed alongside the prospective product purpose and final M4 target. Publicly authored histories gain no held-out status from that purpose. Compatibility with inherited M4 requirements, assertion relevance and sufficiency require the independent complete scope review. V2 amendments, browser behavior, CLI behavior and raw HTTP observations are not inferred from these finite M2 histories.

The shared final acceptance adapter is a separate integration owner. The recipe packets pin the actual shared `ObservationSpec` implementation as well as scope ancestors, compiler and consumer sources, so changed final routing requires a fresh combined qualification of the integrated bytes. A successful recipe or fixture owner does not establish final acceptance, physical runtime admission, created/prestart sandbox qualification, whole-project coverage or model quality. Existing unchanged qualification receipts are retained as historical evidence under their original contracts.

Contributor test receipts and independent source review will be pinned in the final handoff. Central verification, any physical candidate qualification and the complete rehearsal remain the root verification owner's separate work.
