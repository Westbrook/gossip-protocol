# Candidate scope consumer v1

This is a prospective controller integration boundary. It consumes a complete
compiled design only after the trusted controller authenticates the separate
scope, admission and control prerequisites. It does not supply the real
production ScopePlan, independently reviewed check mapping, qualification
recipes, six-trajectory controller or statistical results.

The synthetic tests qualify consumer behavior. Their callbacks are explicitly
simulated authorities and cannot establish that an independent review or
physical qualification occurred. The release bridge additionally consumes an
actual controller journal; its authority extends only to that journal's narrow
registered release gate.

## Controller ownership

`CandidateScopeConsumer(EvidenceAuthority).assess(inventory, declaration, scope,
subject)` accepts design inputs and an exact final subject. There is no receipt
upload, passed flag, caller-supplied expected hash or callback argument to
`assess`. The trusted host separately installs the authority capability before
candidate submission. Its `AuthoritySnapshot` owns or authenticates original
registrations, execution journals, review and promotion receipts, the terminal
cohort barrier and externally retained checkpoints.

The snapshot supplies `RegisteredAcceptance` from its independent prospective
registration, including the inventory, scope, whole declaration, registry design,
execution contract and qualification specifications. Matching these hashes binds
identity; it does not establish authority. A fabricated Python implementation of
`EvidenceAuthority` is outside the trusted-host boundary. This component is not a
cryptographic attestation service, nor does subclassing its interface qualify a
provider. Production installation must implement each authentication method
against independently retained originals and protect that capability from
candidate execution.

The consumer checks the snapshot's retained checkpoint before consumption, after
prerequisites and at the end. The implementation must reject rollback, appended
or changed evidence and revocation. An unavailable authority yields an explicit
incomplete result. A changed checkpoint or substituted provenance is invalid
evidence, never a demonstrated candidate defect.

## Exact qualification binding

The compiler still must cover all 123 product requirements and the three
separate prerequisites. The recognized v2 inventory retains 312 source
obligations, 22 qualification authorities and 188 inherited gap notes. B06's
eight affected IDs are not a smaller acceptance denominator.

Every prerequisite physical gate gets an independently registered
`QualificationSpec`. Its request binds the entire registration, complete
prerequisite suite and execution gate, and all prospective product gate
lineages: requirement/case mapping, ordered suite, evaluator, runtime/image,
environment, limits, seed, execution protocol and purpose. The complete
declaration also pins original definition purposes, compatibility and approved
prospective purpose assignments. Qualification source identity remains the
separate harness subject; candidate outputs cannot be substituted for it.

The required facets remain distinct:

- `scope`: independent semantic review of inventory completeness, clause and
  selector coverage, applicability, original purposes and scope disclosures.
  Digest equality or successful compiler construction does not establish this.
- `admission`: authenticated checks of the actual declared evaluator's input,
  output, isolation and rejection boundaries, bound to its exact target suites
  and runtime. Product behavior remains separately required.
- `semantic_conflict`, `ancestry`, `package_review` and `integration_review`:
  authentication of the authored clean textual merge/repair control and both
  appropriate reviewer decisions.

CONTROL registers a structured `ControlRecipe`: common base, two branch
commit/tree/source inventories and disjoint changed paths, actual merged and
repaired revisions, exact before/after public probe contracts, expected failing
before and passing after observations, and both source-bound reviewer identities,
roles, decisions and definitions. The authority must inspect actual Git ancestry,
changes, clean merge, original public observations and reviews. A structurally
valid recipe or four passed labels supplies none of that authentication.
Successful detection of the defective control qualifies the harness; it is not
counted as a failed model-produced candidate.

`QualificationEvidence` retains original execution and verifier references,
source, purpose, ordered case outcomes, separate facet outcomes and physical or
reused mode. Missing cases/facets, skipped checks, infrastructure errors and known
failed qualification assertions remain distinct. One execution ID or original
receipt cannot substitute for two separately declared physical slots.

The frozen SCOPE, CONTROL and ADMISSION gate policies permit exact qualified
reuse. This consumer permits authenticated complete passing originals with an
explicit authenticated reuse receipt and unchanged complete request lineage.
It does not relabel original purposes. Qualification execution purposes are
closed to `harness_qualification` and `registry_qualification`; product purposes
such as `independent_acceptance` and `repeatability` require a separate adapter
with a frozen-cohort chronology contract and are rejected here, physical or
reused. Original public-development receipts cannot be renamed public-release
receipts to pass this boundary.

## Product and final barriers

Any missing, failed or invalid prerequisite prevents product observation
consumption. Passing prerequisites cannot discharge `M1-ARCH-01`,
`M1-INTEGRATION-01` or the three affected ADAPTER product rows. Their candidate
package/delegation/workflow/integration and actual physical behavior remains
bound to the exact final source in the complete product Registry.

Product observations, promotion and freeze also come only from the trusted
snapshot. Independent product observation requests require the complete six
trajectory barrier with the exact execution contract, product, final milestone
and own source. The observation authority must authenticate original dispatch
and outcome provenance, plus actual after-freeze chronology; a matching freeze
receipt hash alone is insufficient. The generic registry then enforces complete
ordered outcomes, fresh independent/repeatability observations, exact public
reuse, promotion and the final barrier.

Unavailable or invalid sibling observations and unavailable promotion do not
erase already authenticated candidate failures. The outer result retains issues
separately from any partial product assessment, and neither the outer nor nested
assessment may claim acceptance when an issue exists. Only
`ConsumerResult.accepted_against_registry` with no issues and the complete
product assessment can report acceptance. No production authority installation
or actual whole-project acceptance is supplied by these tests.

## Concrete release journal bridges

`ReleaseObservationSource` accepts the exact v1
`CandidateReleaseExecution` owner and its separately retained
`ControllerCheckpoint`. `V2ReleaseObservationSource` accepts their exact v2
counterparts. V1 objects cannot be cast into the v2 contract. Both require the
requested gate to equal the execution registration, check the external
checkpoint, call `verified_execution()` against original controller-owned raw
observations, and require the checkpoint to remain unchanged. Their original
purpose and source binding remain intact. Independent use also verifies the
execution owner's exact cohort capability.

These bridges read completed originals without redispatch. The checkpoint must
have been retained after verifier publication; an uncheckpointed verifier suffix
is rejected. Fixture journals and candidate-appended self-consistent success
records cannot yield authenticated product evidence. The bridges do not mint
scope, admission, semantic control, reviewer, promotion or cohort qualifications.
The v2 physical release tests exercise the bridge on real completed positive and
emitted-package defect journals, separately from synthetic consumer tests.

Required next steps remain the real independently reviewed full scope mapping,
concrete qualification and study authorities, all mandatory candidate suites,
full cohort/recovery rehearsal, prospectively frozen study and fresh final
observations. This boundary supplies engineering safeguards, not comparative
samples or statistical evidence for a swarm advantage.
