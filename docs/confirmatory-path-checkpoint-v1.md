# Path from harness checks to comparative evidence

This source audit records the investigation state after cycle 6, based on commit
`0d2d7cee7f9941a247bd6d61b9914a27eb1f132b`. It does not report a new experiment,
change an execution contract, authorize spending, or select a statistical method
or sample size. The accompanying
[machine-readable checkpoint](../analysis/confirmatory-path-checkpoint-v1.json)
binds every retained source by SHA256 and maps numerical claims to exact JSON
pointers. Generated `runs/` and `results/` evidence is retained locally and may
not be available in a fresh clone; a hash reference does not supply its contents.

The user's goal is better completed software without agents getting lost or
stopping. Cost and speed are secondary. The proposed **+10 percentage point**
worthwhile-effect threshold below is an engineering recommendation from the
existing design, not a threshold requested or approved by the user.

Coverage is the retained equal-project-weight mean, rounded to one decimal

## Shortest credible sequence

1. **Run the existing controller pilot once its funding and exact-contract gates
   permit.** Its 12 trajectories are warehouse/job-queue × two repetitions ×
   current-independent, improved-independent and improved-sequential. The full
   scripted rehearsal was independently certified, but contained no provider
   calls and supplies no model-quality results. The live pilot is exploratory:
   four matched blocks still repeat only two development families. Shared
   first-milestone A/B candidates and common scouts are declared shared setup,
   not independent end-to-end replication. Its controller contrast measures the
   routing/retention package; its formation contrast measures independent versus
   serial generation under the improved controller. Neither measures gossip.
2. **Freeze the chosen controller, then freeze a held-out family registry and
   confirmatory design.** The inspected plans require such a registry but do not
   contain an executable, frozen confirmatory roster. Exclude Graph/Calendar and
   their derivatives, warehouse/job-queue, and all other fixture families used
   for development or policy selection. New filenames or repositories do not
   establish independence. Under the current proposed analysis, each independent
   family contributes one matched pair with fresh model draws; related variants
   and repetitions remain a cluster. A different clustered or stratified design
   needs its own prospectively specified estimator and uncertainty analysis.
3. **Complete the distributed project loop and the separate four-cell study.**
   The primary system contrast is peer decisions plus gossip (P-G) versus durable
   orchestration plus replicated broker (O-B). The O-G and P-B bridge arms
   distinguish dissemination and decision placement. All four need the same
   logical policy, model interface, common safety services, offered resources,
   credible failover, and independently assigned exogenous fault schedule.
   The existing proposal calls for unseen multi-module projects with at least
   three cumulative milestones. Its family count, fault-mixture weights,
   placement/resource contract and preservation decision rule remain to be
   frozen. A two-arm alternative would be a different prospectively approved
   protocol, not completion of the existing four-cell scope.
4. **Close a complete fixed cohort before private evaluation and inference.**
   Retain failed and stopped trajectories. Do not replace difficult outcomes,
   continue only cheap arms, reuse private results to repair active candidates,
   or extend the sample after inspecting efficacy. An interrupted or incomplete
   cohort cannot receive the proposed confirmatory declaration.

## Decision and uncertainty

The proposed primary outcome is independently accepted **whole-project**
completion: an exact source completes the public policy within the common active
deadline and offered budget, is frozen, then passes all cumulative inherited and
new-feature holdout gates and valid promotion after the cohort barrier. A source
that stops early remains unsuccessful through the horizon. Report active source
freeze, cohort waiting, private adjudication and final promotion separately.

Keep inherited correctness inside the all-or-nothing acceptance gate. A separate
preservation co-primary claim would require a predeclared rule and appropriate
error allocation. Safety violations remain protocol failures. Neither coverage
nor lower cost should compensate for an invalid release.

For the proposed +10 point practical margin, apply the prospectively chosen
paired acceptance-difference interval:

| Interval result | Permitted conclusion |
| --- | --- |
| Lower endpoint exceeds +10 points | Supports worthwhile benefit within the tested population and contract |
| Upper endpoint is below +10 points | Rules out that worthwhile benefit within the tested population and contract |
| Otherwise | Inconclusive |

A negative upper endpoint is stronger evidence of harm. Rejecting zero with
McNemar's test is weaker than establishing a gain greater than +10 points.
The margin, confidence method, sample size, stopping rule and multiplicity
allocation remain proposals until frozen before confirmatory outcomes. If a
single research claim can succeed on either formation or live-protocol results,
the existing design proposes allocating alpha .025 to each; it does not permit
choosing whichever study looks better afterward.

## How much confirmation would require

The existing planning grid uses a conservative simultaneous
Bonferroni–Clopper–Pearson interval for the two discordant-cell probabilities.
The following values are copied from that retained grid, not recalculated or
validated anew in this audit. They assume independently sampled family pairs
and alpha .025 per claim.

| Independent family pairs | Establish >+10 points when true gain is +20 points, discordance 40% | Rule out +10 points when true gain is zero, discordance 30% |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 100 | 4.0% | 5.6% |
| 200 | 16.0% | 21.7% |
| 400 | 51.3% | 62.6% |
| 800 | 92.5% | 96.8% |

These are two different planning scenarios, not outcome probabilities inferred
from our small pilot. **800 pairs is an example, not an established minimum or
selected sample size.** A true effect exactly at the +10 point boundary should
usually remain inconclusive; detecting that effect against zero is a different
question. More scenarios from the same family cannot satisfy the independent
family requirement.

The retained historical illustration is **$2.125539 per two-arm pair**, or
**$1,700.4312 at 800 pairs**. It divides the eight matched-policy trajectories'
API charges by four pairs, excluding strong anchors. It is neither a current
price quote nor a forecast, includes no fixture/engineering/local-compute cost,
and cannot price the four-cell topology study. That study needs its own cost
projection. [Source: confirmatory planning grid and budget](../analysis/benchmark-confirmatory-design-v4.json).

Before requesting confirmation-scale funding, independently qualify a more
efficient established paired-difference interval and recompute power under the
same assumptions. Newcombe describes paired profile-likelihood and score-based
alternatives; exact2x2 documents a paired difference interval based on melding.
These are **candidates for later qualification**, not methods adopted here.
Sparse/boundary coverage, numerical implementation and behavior at the nonzero
margin must be assessed before selection, with the method fixed before outcomes.
[Newcombe's paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9839354/) and
[official exact2x2 documentation](https://search.r-project.org/CRAN/refmans/exact2x2/html/mcnemarExactDP.html).

The concrete scientific gaps are a frozen held-out registry, selected sample
size and method, a frozen target fault mixture, and the final preservation/error
allocation rule. The engineering gaps include qualified financial dispatch,
integrated sandboxed candidate execution and Git promotion, source selection and
repair/reopening, and a complete independent final-evaluation barrier. This audit
does not close those gaps or claim statistical proof for or against the technique.
