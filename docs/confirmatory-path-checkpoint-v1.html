Decision and uncertainty

The proposed primary outcome is independently accepted whole-project completion: an exact source completes the public policy within the common active deadline and offered budget, is frozen, then passes all cumulative inherited and new-feature holdout gates and valid promotion after the cohort barrier. A source that stops early remains unsuccessful through the horizon. Report active source freeze, cohort waiting, private adjudication and final promotion separately.

Keep inherited correctness inside the all-or-nothing acceptance gate. A separate preservation co-primary claim would require a predeclared rule and appropriate error allocation. Safety violations remain protocol failures. Neither coverage nor lower cost should compensate for an invalid release.

For the proposed +10 point practical margin, apply the prospectively chosen paired acceptance-difference interval:

Interval result Permitted conclusion
Lower endpoint exceeds +10 points Supports worthwhile benefit within the tested population and contract
Upper endpoint is below +10 points Rules out that worthwhile benefit within the tested population and contract
Otherwise Inconclusive

A negative upper endpoint is stronger evidence of harm. Rejecting zero with McNemar's test is weaker than establishing a gain greater than +10 points. The margin, confidence method, sample size, stopping rule and multiplicity allocation remain proposals until frozen before confirmatory outcomes. If a single research claim can succeed on either formation or live-protocol results, the existing design proposes allocating alpha .025 to each; it does not permit choosing whichever study looks better afterward.

How much confirmation would require

The existing planning grid uses a conservative simultaneous Bonferroni–Clopper–Pearson interval for the two discordant-cell probabilities. The following values are copied from that retained grid, not recalculated or validated anew in this audit. They assume independently sampled family pairs and alpha .025 per claim.

Independent family pairs Establish >+10 points when true gain is +20 points, discordance 40% Rule out +10 points when true gain is zero, discordance 30%
100 4.0% 5.6%
200 16.0% 21.7%
400 51.3% 62.6%
800 92.5% 96.8%

These are two different planning scenarios, not outcome probabilities inferred from our small pilot. 800 pairs is an example, not an established minimum or selected sample size. A true effect exactly at the +10 point boundary should usually remain inconclusive; detecting that effect against zero is a different question. More scenarios from the same family cannot satisfy the independent family requirement.

The retained historical illustration is $2.125539 per two-arm pair, or $1,700.4312 at 800 pairs. It divides the eight matched-policy trajectories' API charges by four pairs, excluding strong anchors. It is neither a current price quote nor a forecast, includes no fixture/engineering/local-compute cost, and cannot price the four-cell topology study. That study needs its own cost projection. Source: confirmatory planning grid and budget.

Before requesting confirmation-scale funding, independently qualify a more efficient established paired-difference interval and recompute power under the same assumptions. Newcombe describes paired profile-likelihood and score-based alternatives; exact2x2 documents a paired difference interval based on melding. These are candidates for later qualification, not methods adopted here. Sparse/boundary coverage, numerical implementation and behavior at the nonzero margin must be assessed before selection, with the method fixed before outcomes. Newcombe's paper and official exact2x2 documentation.
